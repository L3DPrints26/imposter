Never played? It takes two minutes to learn.

This is word mode, the one you play sitting down. Six lines and you know the game.

  1. 1
    Sit in a circle One person holds the box to start with. It gets passed around.
  2. 2
    Pick your numbers How many of you are playing, how many imposters, and whether the imposter gets a hint. Three screens, then tap START.
  3. 3
    Pass it round The box names the next player. Hold the button, read your card, let go, tap the arrow. Keep going until everyone has looked.
  4. 4
    Now you know Crew all saw the same secret word. Imposters saw the word IMPOSTER and one hint instead. Everyone saw the category.
  5. 5
    One clue each The box says who starts. Go round the circle and say one word about the secret word. One word, out loud, no explaining it.
  6. 6
    Vote, then tap Reveal Talk it over, then point at once on three. Most votes is out. Reveal tells you the word and who was lying.
The only three rules for your clue

Do not say the secret word. Do not be so obvious that the imposter works it out. Do not be so vague that everyone starts looking at you.

Nothing happening when you tap? It is locked, not broken

There is a padlock at the top of the screen. While it is shut, nothing else on the screen answers, so the box cannot get tapped by accident while it is being passed. Tap the padlock and it all works again. How the lock works →

Quick tips and how to play for tag mode

Tag mode is the interactive one. Same game, but you are outside and running instead of sitting down, and there is no secret word. The box just hands out the roles.

  • Play somewhere with things to hide behind. An open field makes every tag obvious.
  • Imposters take crew out by tagging them. Get tagged and you fall over and stay down, so somebody finds you.
  • Found a body? Shout, and everyone comes in. Those meetings are free and you get as many as you want.
  • No body, but you want everyone in anyway? Call an emergency meeting on the box. There is a limited number and all of you share them.
  • End every meeting by counting "3, 2, 1, UP!" so anyone faking a death stands up at the same moment. Then nobody can tag for 15 seconds.
  • Tap the cooldown tile the second you tag someone, and let it run down before you tag again.

Word mode

Sit down, pass it round, find the liar.

Everyone sees the same secret word. The imposter only gets a hint, and has to fake it.

  1. 1
    Set it up Choose players, then imposters, then the hint switch. Then press START.
  2. 2
    Pass it round The box says who it goes to next. That player taps the arrow, holds HOLD to see their card, lets go, and taps Next.
  3. 3
    One clue each The box picks who starts. Go round the circle, one word out loud. Then talk, then vote, then tap Reveal to see the answer.

Reading your card

Crew see the word. Imposters see IMPOSTER and a line that starts Hint: with a related word after it, never the answer. Turn the hint off in setup and the imposter gets only the category, which makes their job much harder.

Every hint is built to let you bluff, not to let you guess. On Dog you might get collar, walk or loyal. Enough to say something that fits, never enough to know it is a dog.

Everyone can see the category. That part is not secret. It is what gives the imposter a chance.

Why you hold the button

The card only shows while you hold the button. Let go and it hides, so you cannot leave it showing by accident. Before you hold it, the card itself tells you to.

Bluffing

You know the category and one related word. Say something vague enough to get away with, but solid enough that nobody suspects you.

The whole game in one line

Too obvious and the imposter learns the word. Too vague and everyone thinks you're the imposter.

The vote

Talk it over, then point at once on three. Most votes is out. The device does not run the vote. Arguing about it is half the fun.

Then settle it

Once you have voted, tap Reveal. The box has known the answer the whole time and this is where it tells you: the word, and exactly which players were lying. No arguing about who was actually the imposter.

Tag mode

Same game, but outside and running.

Play somewhere with stuff to hide behind. An open field makes every tag obvious. There is no secret word in this mode. The device just hands out roles.

Tagging

Imposters take crew out by tagging them. If you get tagged, fall over and stay down so somebody finds the body. No wandering off.

Ghosts

Dead players go quiet, leave the play area, and never point at their killer.

Fake deaths

Both sides can use them. Crew drop next to someone they suspect. Imposters drop right after a tag to muddy who did it.

The two kinds of meeting

  • Body meeting. Anyone who finds a body shouts, and everyone gathers. You get as many of these as you want. They do not use up the count on the device.
  • Emergency meeting. Called when there is no body. There is a limited number, shared by everyone, and imposters can call one to look innocent.
End every meeting the same way

Count out "3, 2, 1, UP!" so everyone faking a death stands up at the same time. Otherwise the faker is obvious. Then nobody can tag for 15 seconds, so people can spread out.

The lock

So nobody taps their way into your word.

A touchscreen going round a table gets tapped by accident. By the next player's thumb, or by the table itself when somebody sets it down. The padlock is there to stop that.

What the padlock does

It sits at the top of the screen, just left of the home icon. While it is shut, nothing else on the screen answers you. Holding the button will not bring the word up. The arrow will not move you on. Tap the padlock and everything works again.

Read this before somebody thinks it is broken

A locked screen looks exactly like a frozen one. It is not. Look at the top of the screen, tap the padlock, and carry on.

Off, Manual, Auto

The Lock tile in settings cycles through all three.

  • Off. No padlock at all, and nothing ever locks.
  • Manual. The padlock is there and you shut it and open it yourself.
  • Auto. The box locks itself the moment you tap Next and hand it on. The next player taps the padlock, sees their word, taps Next, and it locks again behind them. Nobody can thumb their way into somebody else's word on the way across.
It only locks while the cards go round

Once everyone has looked, it stops. The talk screen and the tag play screen are shared, and locking those would just be in the way. A fresh round never starts locked.

Settings

Every knob on the device.

Players
3 to 15.
Imposters
Half the players, at most. So 8 players allows 4.
Hint word
Word mode, in setup. On: the imposter gets a related word. Off: only the category. The switch says which, so you are never guessing what it does.
Meetings
Tag mode, in setup. The shared pool of emergency meetings, up to nine, counted down on the play screen during a round. Body meetings are free and do not touch it.
Light
Screen brightness, ten steps.
Theme
Looks to pick from, three on screen at a time with arrows to page through the rest. Paper is the bright one, and it is the one to use outside.
Tips
The small lines that tell you what a screen wants. Leave them on for your first few games and turn them off once you know the box.
Lock
Off, Manual or Auto. Manual puts a padlock at the top of the screen that you shut yourself. Auto shuts it every time the box is handed on. While it is shut, nothing answers except the padlock. All of it here →
Home and settings
Top right of every screen once a game is under way, and on the menu the settings sliders sit in the middle of the top row instead. You are never more than one tap from either, and Back out of settings returns you to the screen you left.
Rules
Top left of the menu. Six short pages for whichever mode you tapped last, and this page as a QR code on the sixth.
Battery
A percentage in settings, and a red badge on the menu once it is down to 20. The box dims after a minute of nobody touching it and blanks after ten, and a tap wakes it right where it was. It will not do either in the middle of a tag cooldown, because that screen is the game at that moment.
Off
Top right of the menu. Tapping it turns the backlight off and blanks the screen, and a tap on the glass brings it back exactly where you left it. There is no power switch on this one, so that is what off means here. It is still sipping a little in the background, so plug it in between game nights the way you would anything else with a battery in it.
Reveal
End of a round. In word mode it shows the word and which players were lying. In tag mode there is no word, so it just names the imposters.
The five tiles behind the sliders

Light, Theme, Battery, Tips and Lock. That is the whole settings page, and it is one page, not a list you scroll. Players, imposters, the hint switch and meetings are not in here, because you set those on the way into a game instead.

The word bank

Everyday categories, the sort a room full of people all know without explaining: animals, food, places, sports, jobs, school and plenty more. Every word carries five possible hints and each hint is a single word, so nothing gets cut off on a screen this small. The box will not deal a word it has already dealt in the last 16 rounds.

Update your box

Plug it into a computer and update it here.

New words and fixes go out through this page. Nothing to download, nothing to install on your computer, no account to make.

What you need

  • A laptop or a desktop running Chrome or Edge. Firefox cannot do this and neither can Safari.
  • A USB-C cable that carries data, not just power. A charging cable looks exactly the same and does nothing here. If you have one that came with a phone or a pair of headphones, use that one.
  • The box, awake, with something on the screen.
It will not work on a phone

There is no way round it. An iPhone cannot do this at all, and Safari cannot do it on anything. Go and borrow a laptop. Once you are sat at one the whole thing takes about a minute.

This browser cannot talk to the box. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer and the button will be here. This page has to be opened over a secure connection for the button to work.

There is nothing new to put on it right now. When there is, the button turns up here on its own.

What an update actually does

It puts the newest version of the game on the box. Your brightness, your theme, your tips switch and your lock setting all stay exactly as you had them, because they sit in a part of the memory the update steps over. There is nothing else on the box that is yours to lose. It keeps no scores, no names and no saved games.

If nothing happens

Try a different cable first, that is nearly always what it is. Then try the other socket on the computer. If the window opens but the box is not in the list, the cable you are using only carries power.
