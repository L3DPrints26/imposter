Word mode

Sit down, pass it round, find the liar.

Everyone sees the same secret word. The imposter only gets a hint, and has to fake it.

  1. 1
    Set it up Choose players and imposters, then press START.
  2. 2
    Pass it round Hold the button to see your card. Let go before you hand it on.
  3. 3
    One clue each Go round the circle, one word out loud. Then talk, then vote.

Reading your card

Crew see the word. Imposters get a hint word instead. It is related, but it is not the answer. Turn the hint off in setup to make their job much harder.

Everyone can see the category. That part is not secret. It is what gives the imposter a chance.

Why you hold the button

The word only shows while you hold the button. Let go and it hides, so you cannot leave it showing by accident.

What the imposter sees

Bluffing

You know the category and one related word. Say something vague enough to get away with, but solid enough that nobody suspects you.

The whole game in one line

Too obvious and the imposter learns the word. Too vague and everyone thinks you're the imposter.

The vote

Talk it over, then point at once on three. Most votes is out. The device does not run the vote. Arguing about it is half the fun.

Crew win

Vote out the imposter.

Imposter wins

Survive the vote, or guess the word.

Interactive mode

Same game, but outside and running.

Play somewhere with stuff to hide behind. An open field makes every tag obvious. There is no secret word in this mode. The device just hands out roles.

Tagging

Imposters take crew out by tagging them. If you get tagged, fall over and stay down so somebody finds the body. No wandering off.

Ghosts

Dead players go quiet, leave the play area, and never point at their killer.

Fake deaths

Both sides can use them. Crew drop next to someone they suspect. Imposters drop right after a tag to muddy who did it.

The two kinds of meeting

  • Body meeting. Anyone who finds a body shouts, and everyone gathers. You get as many of these as you want. They do not use up the count on the device.
  • Emergency meeting. Called when there is no body. There is a limited number, shared by everyone, and imposters can call one to look innocent.
End every meeting the same way

Count out "3, 2, 1, UP!" so everyone faking a death stands up at the same time. Otherwise the faker is obvious. Then nobody can tag for 15 seconds, so people can spread out.
