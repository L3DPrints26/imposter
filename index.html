Word mode

Sitting down, five minutes, no setup beyond passing the box.

Dealing the roles

  1. Set players and impostersThen press START. The device picks a random category and word.
  2. Pass the box roundOne person at a time. Hold the button to see your card, let go before you hand it on.
  3. Read your cardCrew see the secret word. Imposters see a hint word instead — related, but not the answer.
Why holding matters

The word is only on screen while a finger is held down, so it can never be left showing when the box changes hands.

Everyone can see the category. That part isn't secret — it's what gives the imposter a fighting chance. Turn the hint word off in setup to make their job much harder.

Giving clues

Go round the circle. Each person says one word out loud that relates to the secret word. Not a sentence. Not a definition.

The tension

Too obvious and the imposter learns the word. Too vague and the crew think you're the imposter. That squeeze is the whole game.

The vote

Talk it over, then point at once on a count of three. Most votes is out. The device deliberately doesn't run the vote — arguing about it is half the fun.

Crew win

Vote out the imposter.

Imposter wins

Survive the vote, or guess the word.

Interactive mode

Outside, on your feet. Best somewhere with things to hide behind — an open field makes every tag obvious.

Tagging

Imposters take crew out by tagging them. If you're tagged you fall over and commit — lie there so somebody finds the body. No wandering off.

The two meetings

  • Body meeting — anyone who finds a body shouts and everyone gathers. Unlimited, and it doesn't use up the device's count.
  • Emergency meeting — called with no body. Limited, shared by everyone, and imposters can call one to look innocent.
End every meeting the same way

Count out "3, 2, 1, UP!" so anyone faking a death stands at the same moment. Without it, the faker is instantly obvious. Then nobody may tag for 15 seconds, so people can scatter.

Ghosts

Dead players go silent, leave the play area, and never point at their killer.

Fake deaths

Both sides can use them. Crew drop next to someone they suspect so others vote them out. Imposters drop right after a tag to muddy who did it.

Reference

What every setting on the device does.

Players
3 to 15.
Imposters
Capped at half the players — 8 players allows at most 4.
Hint word
Off makes the imposter's job much harder.
Meetings
The shared pool of emergency meetings. Body meetings are free.
Light
Screen brightness, ten steps.
Theme
Night, Gold or Paper. Paper is the one to use outdoors.
Touch
Re-runs the three-dot calibration if taps ever drift.
The word bank

375 words across 15 categories, each with five possible hints — 1,875 in total. Every word, hint and category is a single word, so nothing gets cut off on the screen.
