Handheld party game

IMPOSTER

Everything the little screen doesn't have room for. Two ways to play: one round a table, one running around outside.

Word mode Interactive mode Settings

Word mode

Sitting down, 3–15 players, about five minutes a round.

Dealing

Set the players and imposters, press START, then pass the box around. Each person holds the button to see their card and lets go before handing it on. The word is only on screen while a finger is held down, so it can't be left showing.

  • Crew see the secret word.
  • Imposters see one hint word instead — related, but not the answer.
  • Turn the hint off in setup to make it much harder for them.

Everyone can see the category. That part isn't secret — it's what gives the imposter a fighting chance.

Giving clues

Go round the circle. Each person says one word out loud that relates to the secret word. Not a sentence. Not a definition.

The tension

Too obvious and the imposter learns the word. Too vague and the crew think you're the imposter. That squeeze is the whole game.

Voting

Talk it over, then point at once on a count of three. Most votes is out. The device deliberately doesn't run the vote — arguing about it is half the fun.

Imposter winsSurvive the vote, or guess the word.

Interactive mode

Outside, on your feet. Best somewhere with things to hide behind — an open field makes every tag obvious.

Tagging

Imposters take crew out by tagging them. If you're tagged you fall over and commit — lie there so somebody finds the body. No wandering off.

Two kinds of meeting

  • Body meeting — anyone who finds a body shouts and everyone gathers. Unlimited, and it doesn't use up the device's count.
  • Emergency meeting — called with no body. Limited, shared by everyone, and imposters can call one to look innocent.

Every meeting ends with a group "3, 2, 1, UP!" so anyone faking a death stands at the same moment. Without that, the faker is instantly obvious.

Nobody may tag for 15 seconds after a meeting ends, so people can scatter.

Ghosts

Dead players go silent, leave the play area, and never point at their killer.

Fake deaths

Both sides can use them. Crew drop next to someone they suspect so others vote them out. Imposters drop right after a tag to muddy who did it.

Settings

  • Players — 3 to 15.
  • Imposters — capped at half the players, so 8 players allows at most 4.
  • Hint word — off makes the imposter's job much harder.
  • Meetings — the shared pool of emergency meetings. Body meetings are free.
  • Light, Theme, Battery, Touch — behind the sliders icon on the menu. Touch re-runs the three-dot calibration if taps ever drift.

Words

375 words across 15 categories, each with five possible hints — 1,875 in total. Every word, hint and category is a single word, so nothing gets cut off on the screen.

Built by GamerCrafter15. The device picks a random category every round — hold the button, read your card, pass it on.
