The menu

Word mode

Sitting down, five minutes, one liar.

Everyone sees the same secret word. The imposter sees a hint instead, and has to bluff their way through.

  1. Set it upChoose players and imposters, then press START. The device picks a random category and word.
  2. Pass it roundOne person at a time. Hold the button to see your card, let go before you hand it on.
  3. Give one clue eachGo round the circle. One word out loud. Then talk, then vote.

Reading your card

Crew see the word. Imposters see a hint word instead — related, but not the answer. Turn the hint off in setup to make their job much harder.

Everyone can see the category. That part isn't secret — it's what gives the imposter a fighting chance.

Why you hold the button

The word is only on screen while a finger is held down, so it can never be left showing when the box changes hands.

Bluffing

You know the category and one related word. Say something vague enough to survive and specific enough to sound like you know the answer.

The tension

Too obvious and the imposter learns the word. Too vague and the crew think you're the imposter. That squeeze is the whole game.

The vote

Talk it over, then point at once on a count of three. Most votes is out. The device deliberately doesn't run the vote — arguing about it is half the fun.

Crew win

Vote out the imposter.

Imposter wins

Survive the vote, or guess the word.

Interactive mode

Outside, on your feet.

Best somewhere with things to hide behind — an open field makes every tag obvious. No word here: the device just deals roles.

Tagging

Imposters take crew out by tagging them. If you're tagged you fall over and commit — lie there so somebody finds the body. No wandering off.

Ghosts

Dead players go silent, leave the play area, and never point at their killer.

Fake deaths

Both sides can use them. Crew drop next to someone they suspect. Imposters drop right after a tag to muddy who did it.

The two meetings

  • Body meeting — anyone who finds a body shouts and everyone gathers. Unlimited, and it doesn't use up the device's count.
  • Emergency meeting — called with no body. Limited, shared by everyone, and imposters can call one to look innocent.
End every meeting the same way

Count out "3, 2, 1, UP!" so anyone faking a death stands at the same moment. Without it the faker is obvious. Then nobody may tag for 15 seconds, so people can scatter.

Tag cooldown

Honour system — the device doesn't time it. Add about 10 seconds per extra imposter.
