Fix touch on your box

Use this if the screen isn't where you're tapping, or taps land on the wrong things. It takes about two minutes, and you can't make things worse: the reset button always brings back the factory setting.

Open this in Chrome or Edge

This page talks to your box through the browser. Chrome and Edge on a computer can do it. Phones, tablets, Firefox and Safari can't.

1. Plug the box into this computer

  1. Use a USB-C cable that carries data. A charging-only cable won't work.
  2. Turn the box on. The screen should light up.
  3. Press the button below, then pick the box from the list.

2. Pick one

Reset to factory is the safe choice. It puts back the setting every box shipped with, and it's the one to try first.

Calibrate by hand is for when the reset isn't enough. You'll tap three dots on the box.

Tap the three dots on the box

Tap each dot once with a finger, in order. The guide below shows where they are.
